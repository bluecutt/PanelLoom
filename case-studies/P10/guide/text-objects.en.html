Independent Lettered Objects

中文 | English

P10 uses 10 lettered objects. Exteriors are transparent; bubble interiors remain white. Connected bubbles can move as one group. The two thought groups requiring separate placement have been split.

The style guide pairs each asset type with real prompt excerpts for contour, connections, tails, size, emotional ink gestures, and transparency. English meanings below are reading aids; the Chinese artwork and exact strings remain unchanged.
