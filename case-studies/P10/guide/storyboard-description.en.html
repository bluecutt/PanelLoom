English translation of the creator's initial six-part request. The original punctuation and wording are retained in the linked Chinese text. Dialogue below is translated for reading; the Chinese strings in the artwork and prompt records remain unchanged. This describes the request at that point in production. Later changes, including the second unboxed promise and final inset placement, are documented in the workflow and panel design.

1. U01: Close interaction

U01 mainly shows Subaru's shyness and Julius's intense anticipation, focusing on their upper bodies. Subaru occupies the right half of the panel. His body faces left, but his head turns right. His cheeks are lightly flushed, with one bead of sweat from embarrassment. His left index finger scratches his cheek, and he looks a little nervous.

On the right, the dialogue bubbles say “It's not.....that I mind.....”, “But only five minutes,” and “Don't think you can take advantage of me.” Julius's face is very close to Subaru's, almost as if they are about to kiss, creating a sense of approach. He looks happy and relieved to have permission, with a slight blush. His body is already preparing to embrace Subaru. Then a bubble on the right says “Thank you, Subaru.”

2. U02: Details of the embrace

U02 mainly depicts the embrace. Julius wraps his arms around Subaru's waist and buries his head against Subaru's chest, enjoying his presence. Half his profile is visible. His eyes are closed and cheeks lightly flushed, with a contented expression. Subaru strokes his head with one hand. Subaru's cheeks are very red, but his expression is resigned.

Julius stays on the left and Subaru on the right. The camera looks down from the side at approximately 45 degrees. On U02's right are Subaru's thoughts: “.....Here we go again” and “Whenever he makes that face,”. Another thought on the left says “I remember that time.”

3. A01: Young Julius in the corner

I'll provide references for the younger characters in A01 and A02. A01 mainly shows young Julius in a distressed condition. From Subaru's viewpoint, the camera faces him at a downward angle and shows his whole curled posture. He is small and huddled in a corner, arms around his knees. Only one eye is visible, looking up toward young Subaru ahead and above him, outside the frame.

He is dirty, with abrasions on his face and arms. The dark background fades lighter from the frame toward the interior to express his sadness and hardship. On the right is a line spoken by present-day Subaru: “What a troublesome guy.....”.

4. A02: Young Subaru reaching out

A02 mainly shows an extremely cheerful young Subaru. The camera takes the viewpoint of Julius curled on the ground, looking upward from the front at Subaru's body and gesture. Subaru stands before Julius, smiling brightly with his eyes narrowed in happiness. He reaches one hand toward Julius outside the frame, intending to help him stand.

On the left of Subaru's face, add “Become my friend. I'll protect you.” This needs no bubble; it can be placed directly in the artwork later. Subaru is neatly dressed; I'll provide a clothing example. Light rays and stars radiate from Subaru in a bright background, suggesting that he is the light in Julius's life.

5. A03: Profile reaction inset

Remove the previous U04/U03 arrangement, replacing it with a large U03 panel and a small A03 inset on the right. A03 is a close-up of Subaru's profile. His eyes are closed and he looks as if he is complaining, with a hint of reaching his limit, but his cheeks remain flushed. Beside him is a bubble reading “.....Hey”.

6. U03: Chibi ending

The main U03 panel uses chibi versions of Subaru and Julius. It only needs to communicate the overall action, without the detailed embrace of U02. Chibi Julius is on the left, eyes closed, his cheek against Subaru on the right. Both arms extend straight rightward to show the embrace.

Chibi Subaru leans backward on the right. His face is very red; he seems resistant but cannot bring himself to push Julius away, with a flustered defensive gesture. On the right he says “The five minutes are already up, aren't they?!” with strong visual emphasis. On the left Julius says “Let me hold you a little longer.”
