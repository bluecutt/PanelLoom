Opening and embrace redraws use the selected single-panel crops for actions and expressions. Other finished-page references only guide identity and rendering. Prompts constrain framing, placement, hand relationships, background density, and additional canvas space.

Lettered-object prompts specify exact text, reading order, ordinary optical size, emotional ink gestures, bubble shape, and transparency. The creator checks the generated lettering before importing each independent object.

The [style chapter](lettering-styles.en.md) explains how the prompt constraints relate to actual contours, layouts, and emotions. Full prompts remain unchanged. Reusable templates and translations in the guide are clearly separate from historical calls.

The connected permission and single-response call texts match their archived prompt records. The actual combined-thought call contains an additional 99-character English punctuation instruction absent from its archive. This appendix retains the complete call; the active practice assets are the split C03A and C03B.

These are historical materials. For new comics, use your own characters and authorized references, supplying actions, text, and constraints from the selected draft. Historical reference paths belong to the original runtime; external reference files for those paths are not included in this pack.
